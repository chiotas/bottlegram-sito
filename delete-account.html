Delete your account

Bottlegram, by Bulb Mode · Bulb Mode SRL Semplificata, Via Ancona 15, 60035 Jesi (AN), Italy

Your Bottlegram account is anonymous: a random identifier, with no name or email address attached. Deleting it takes your beach and your letters with it, at once.

From the app

  1. Open Bottlegram.
  2. On your beach, tap “Emporium”.
  3. Tap “Leave the sea”, then “Leave forever”.

It works the same on iPhone and on Android, and it is immediate. There is no waiting period and no way back: if you open the app again, you arrive as a new castaway on a new beach, and the app asks your date of birth again.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, write to bottlegram@bulbmode.com with the subject “Leave the sea”. Because accounts have no names, we need you to describe yours so we can find it: the name of your beach, roughly when you arrived, and if you can, the first words of a letter you wrote. We delete it promptly, and in any case within one month, and write back when it is done. If nothing you tell us points to one account, we will say so; we cannot look anyone up by name or email, because we do not have them.

What is deleted

All of this, at once and for good:

  • your anonymous account, your beach and your language;
  • every letter you wrote and every letter you received, including your letters on the beaches of those who received them;
  • your correspondences and the people you blocked;
  • reports made about your letters, together with the copy of the letter they hold;
  • your seal, your ship, and the record of what you bought in the Wreck Emporium;
  • the messages you sent us with “Write to us”, and the name and email address you left there, if any, as the app stored them.

On your phone, the app empties itself: the private names you gave your correspondents, which never left it, go with the rest.

What is kept

  • Reports you made about someone else's letter stay, with nothing that links them to you, so that the decision about that letter stands.
  • If your account had been banished, one random identifier of the deleted account is kept, with nothing else, for as long as the ban stands, so that leaving does not undo it. Your phone keeps the same identifier, in the iCloud Keychain on an iPhone and in Google Block Store on Android.
  • Your “Write to us” messages also arrive in our mailbox as an email, and that copy stays there until you ask us to delete it.
  • Purchases stay in the records of Google Play, or of Apple on an iPhone, which handle the payment. We never see payment details.
  • Backups of the database hold deleted data for up to seven days, then they are overwritten.
  • Crash reports never carried anything that points to you, so there is nothing of yours in them to delete.

What the sea keeps, and for how long, is set out in full on the privacy page.
